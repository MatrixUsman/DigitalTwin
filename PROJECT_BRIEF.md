## What independence means

Each project owns its question, threat model, physical/process assumptions, literature evidence, eligible baseline set, dataset manifests, preprocessing, chronological evaluation protocol, environments, experiment logs, results, limitations, and manuscript. A model or script may eventually be reused when appropriate, but that reuse must be identified and validated separately. One project's calibrated thresholds, learned parameters, test decisions, or performance claims are not evidence for another.

The three papers must investigate substantively distinct questions. Different titles, author names, or dataset names alone do not establish three contributions. Reassess overlap after the baseline audits and before manuscript claims. Record contributions and agree authorship/order separately; the folder-owner mapping does not predetermine either.

Shared items are workflow instructions, the read-only skill package, machine information, and planning conventions. A shared machine does not confer shared dataset permission. Confirm the authorized user(s) and terms for each dataset before making it accessible to other collaborators. No automatic cross-project data links or synchronized result stores are planned.
