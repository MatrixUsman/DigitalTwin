# Papers and reading — Water infrastructure

The three selected manuscripts are downloaded in the [baseline collection](../baselines/README.md), with one folder per paper as requested. CAROTS and IT-DT include arXiv source/bibliography/figures; the ensemble includes publisher and PMC materials. Do not duplicate those files here. The [evidence register](../docs/LITERATURE_EVIDENCE.md) retains source and access limits.

For additional papers outside a baseline pack, keep legally obtained full texts under ignored `papers/downloads/`. Add future reading notes under `papers/notes/`, with exact title/authors/identifier, version/source/access date, relevant pages or sections, method/data/protocol details, limitations, and a distinction between author claims and our observations. Record whether a human actually read a section only when that person says so.

Maintain bibliographic metadata separately from full-text files. Do not redistribute papers or private author materials merely because they are available locally.
