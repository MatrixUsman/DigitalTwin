## Prompts for your own notes

For each reading pass, provide the paper version and actual sections/pages read, then explain:

1. What problem does the paper address, and what does it assume is trustworthy?
2. What enters the method, what does it learn, and what score/alarm does it output?
3. Which equation or stage can you connect to a real author function? Which have no code?
4. Which choices use training, validation, calibration and test information?
5. What changes online, and how could corrupted measurements influence it?
6. What evidence would make you accept or reject the authors' specific claim?

Write unfamiliar terms and disagreements explicitly. An explanation generated by the assistant is not your statement until you review and express your understanding. Do not label any published result as a result we reproduced.
