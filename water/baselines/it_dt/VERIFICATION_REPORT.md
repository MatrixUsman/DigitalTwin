# IT-DT verification report

Audit date: **16 October 2026**. Scope: the collected **arXiv:2603.01621v1** paper, its original source/figures/bibliography, our four study guides and citation, current primary metadata, and a bounded public-code search. This is an AI-assisted evidence audit using the installed ARS `deep-research` fact-check/source-verification guidance under the project's stricter research rules. It is not a human-read declaration, replication, misconduct determination, or certification of every cited paper.

**Decision: the paper and downloaded source are genuine arXiv materials, but IT-DT is not an approved numerical baseline.** Important reporting inconsistencies and missing execution details remain. Preserve it for critical study; do not rely on its reported delay, statistical superiority, or deployment claims without clarification and independent evidence. A real paper can contain errors; neither the errors nor unsuccessful searches establish fabrication.

## Further mathematical and presentation checks

These are reader-derived checks, not benchmark results:

- **Equations 3–6 are dimensionally coherent** for compatible state/input/measurement matrices. Eq. 6 has the usual Gaussian KL direction from the estimated window distribution to the zero-mean reference, provided both covariances are positive definite. The reference covariance's construction still needs the estimator covariance/noise details.
- **Window covariance rank matters.** With WADI dimension 127 and a 60-sample mean-centered window, unregularized sample covariance has rank at most 59. Adding a positive diagonal makes it invertible, but the result depends on units/scaling and regularization. Scaling is not specified. This is not a claim that the regularized formula is undefined.
- **Diagonal regularization does not itself establish Gaussianity.** It conditions a covariance estimate. The statement linking it to mitigation of non-Gaussianity (TeX lines 384–385, p. 6) has no supplied supporting experiment. Reported Mardia/Ljung–Box p-values lack their calculation inputs. A failure to reject a null does not prove normality or model validity.
- **The 99th-percentile rule establishes an empirical calibration target**, not automatically an out-of-sample 1% false-alarm guarantee for dependent windows or shifted operation. The manuscript supplies no finite-sample guarantee accounting for those effects. A thresholded KL score is still a threshold detector.
- **The score depends on fitted first and second moments.** A positive population Gaussian KL when moments differ does not guarantee a finite noisy window will exceed a chosen threshold. Attacks preserving those monitored moments need not be detected. The wording about an assured signal must not be generalized into universal attack detection.
- **Algorithm 1 corrects the state before deciding the alarm.** It does not freeze/reject the suspicious measurement in its state correction and does not specify online parameter, reference-distribution or threshold learning. Our guide correctly distinguishes these operations.
- **The reported speedup is arithmetically plausible as an approximation:** 75.30 / 0.12 = 627.5. This check does not validate either latency. A desktop i9 comparison alone does not demonstrate execution on an industrial edge controller; GPU identity, timing granularity, batch size, warm-up, versions and repetitions are missing.
- **Figure 2's central labels are internally compatible with its stated small variation.** Relative drops from the displayed W=60 maximum are at most about 3.73% (SWaT) and 4.39% (WADI). The plot cannot verify the fold data or shading. The label-access issue in IT-03 remains.
- **Template leftovers are present in the actual public preprint**, not introduced during extraction: title subtitle instructions and a funding-placeholder footnote (TeX lines 20–24; PDF p. 1). These are editorial defects, not evidence of a fake paper.
- **Percentage wording needs care:** precision .910−.885 is 2.5 percentage points and recall .772−.766 is 0.6 percentage points. Relative percentages differ. Do not copy the paper's ambiguous percent wording into our own claims.

Selected support checks:

1. **GDN and TranAD / Attack #41:** the checked primary experiments do not support the canonical-example assertion (IT-04). GDN does support learning sensor relationships and shows a WADI false-reading case. It does not demonstrate that GPU acceleration is a general requirement; the use of particular hardware is a different claim.
2. **TranAD / sensitivity to small anomalies:** the original paper's introduction (pp. 1–2) discusses this issue for simple transformers and presents adversarial training as an attempted remedy. That supports a motivation to test sensitivity; it does not establish IT-DT's specific comparative delay or a universal failure of TranAD.
3. **Otoom / KDE complexity and stealth thresholds:** the [publisher-hosted review PDF](https://jcsra.thestap.com/articles/paper-three.pdf) was inspected (14 pages, especially risk tables/discussion); searches for KDE/kernel/threshold yielded no supporting technical derivation. The paper discusses broad risk and data integrity. It cannot presently substantiate the precise complexity argument attached to it in IT-DT Section II-C.
4. **TWIN-ADAPT / KDE complexity:** the [publisher record and indexed article text](https://www.mdpi.com/1999-5903/16/7/239) identify a continual anomaly-classification framework and drift experiments. Direct full-page/PDF access was intermittent in this audit; the precise KDE-complexity attribution remains **unverified**, not disproved. Do not cite its mere existence as support for that formula.
5. **Ahmad2025 and Mohammed2025 / reconstruction generalization:** the [Ahmad publisher abstract and method description](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-025-11318-3) describe an autoencoder feature transform followed by an MLP classifier; the [Mohammed publisher record](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0316536) verifies the cited paper's identity. Neither metadata nor the checked passages establish the broad proposition that reconstruction necessarily learns statistically plausible attacks as normal. Full claim-level review remains incomplete.
6. **Foundational books/PCA:** [Skogestad's publisher record](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/engineering/electrical-electronics-engineering-10ee/control-systems-technology-10ee3/multivariable-feedback-control-978-0-470-01168-3), [Ding's publisher record](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-540-76304-8), and [Shlens's arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.1100) verify identity. Their existence does not validate this study's identified system, chosen model orders or attack outcomes. These foundational works are background, not recent eligible comparison papers.

No official SWaT/WADI dataset-introduction paper or provider release record appears in the supplied 28-entry bibliography. Provider documentation was therefore consulted independently; benchmark details remain subject to authorized receipt.

## Corrections to our collection and limits

The bibliographic metadata and numbers previously transcribed into our guides match the paper. The guides correctly labelled them as author-reported and code as not located. They nevertheless lacked the figure contradiction, Table I arithmetic question, endpoint mismatch and bibliography findings above. This audit adds visible warnings/links to all four guides, strengthens the execution hold and corrects their bibliography-verification status. **Original PDFs, HTML, TeX, figures and references.bib are untouched.** CITATION.bib required no change.

The authors' actual execution, raw data, honest intent, affiliations, funding, dataset authorization and original generated outputs cannot be authenticated from these materials. No exhaustive publisher-quality, author-identity or retraction investigation was conducted. This audit does not prove every result false or every remaining claim correct. The next bounded action is critical reading and a written list of clarification needs; experimental admission requires independent protocol resolution and executable evidence.
