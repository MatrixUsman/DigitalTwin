# Papers and reading — Smart manufacturing

Full manuscripts have not been downloaded into this project. The [evidence register](../docs/LITERATURE_EVIDENCE.md) identifies sources and access limits; online source inspection is distinct from a local collection.

From the project root, keep legally obtained full texts under ignored `papers/downloads/`. Add future reading notes under `papers/notes/`, with exact title/authors/identifier, version/source/access date, relevant pages or sections, method/data/protocol details, limitations, and a distinction between author claims and our observations. Record whether a human actually read a section only when that person says so.

Maintain bibliographic metadata separately from full-text files. Do not redistribute papers or private author materials merely because they are available locally.
