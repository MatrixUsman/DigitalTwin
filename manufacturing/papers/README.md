# Papers and reading — Smart manufacturing

CASPER's corrected VOR V1.1 has been collected locally; TWINE and CatBoost full manuscripts remain inaccessible through the lawful routes checked. See the [collection/method audit](../docs/MANUSCRIPT_COLLECTION_AUDIT.md) and [artifact manifest](MANUSCRIPT_MANIFEST.json). Selected author code, metadata and a reported table were also collected for static inspection. No upstream program or checkpoint was executed.

From the project root, keep legally obtained full texts under ignored `papers/downloads/` or the selected method's ignored `baselines/<method>/artifacts/`, as this collection does. Add reading notes with exact title/authors/identifier, version/source/access date, relevant pages or sections, method/data/protocol details, limitations, and a distinction between author claims and our observations. Record whether a human actually read a section only when that person says so. The [Step 0 lesson](../docs/RESEARCH_BASICS_LESSON.md) and [unanswered worksheet](../docs/YOUR_STEP_0_NOTES.md) support learning but do not attest comprehension.

Maintain bibliographic metadata separately from full-text files. Do not redistribute papers or private author materials merely because they are available locally.
