# Argument and manuscript scope

**Material:** measured local experiment, primary-source notes and immutable aggregate outputs. **Article type:** a single-acquisition empirical case study. **Venue:** not selected. **Research skill:** bounded academic-paper drafting, source/claim checks and formatting, executed inline; no independent agent panel or automated submission-readiness certification.

Central question: does adding correction discrepancy to an empirical observer's prospective residual help under the frozen normal-calibrated rule? The comparison keeps state evolution fixed, so it does not isolate progression's benefit. The supported answer is a small descriptive improvement with poor absolute detection and competitive simple controls.

Argument order: explain prediction/correction → distinguish close prior art and supervision tracks → specify truthful-telemetry threat and schedule-label limits → define training/state/scoring formulas → disclose selection/calibration/final separation → report all eleven methods and all events → explain event-hit versus point/episode burden → narrow conclusions and specify fresh-evidence requirements.

Working title chosen: **Correction Evidence in Robotic Motion Monitoring: A Controlled CASPER Study**. Alternatives considered: “When Correction Adds Little: Motion Anomaly Detection on CASPER” and “Residuals and Corrections in Robotic Motion Monitoring.” The chosen title is descriptive and less rhetorical. “Controlled” refers to the fixed matched computational comparison, not randomized physical interventions. Title collision/venue fit remains unverified; no claim of unique naming. “Digital twin” is omitted from the title because physical fidelity and general twin benefit are not established.

Contribution language is limited to the explicit score comparison, complete transparent evaluation, and evidence-scope lesson. It does not claim a new estimator, new divergence identity, state-of-the-art accuracy, causal mechanism, operational deployment or general security. The abstract/conclusion follow the measured results. The author-order reply confirms names only; it does not confirm contributions or comprehension.

Supporting teaching document: `../docs/FINDINGS_EXPLAINED_V0.1.md` (relative to manuscript root). The new evidence plan is post-exposure prospective planning, not a pre-CASPER preregistration. No new data or experiments are implied by manuscript drafting.
