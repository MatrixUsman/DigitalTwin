## Finite candidate and explanatory controls

For each joint and time t, fit a local constant-acceleration trajectory to past and current measured q and v. At offsets tau <= 0, position is a + tau*b + tau^2*c/2 and velocity is b + tau*c. Weighted least squares uses training-estimated measurement variances. The output is the endpoint state [a,b], not the mean of past states. Windows are restricted to 5 or 9 samples. Each uses only information available at the score timestamp.

The joint fit is compared with raw measurements, a trailing mean, uncoupled endpoint polynomial fits and a constant-acceleration Kalman filter where its normal-only specification can be justified. A protected command-driven reference remains separate; if residuals are projected instead of observations, the reference's own kinematic defect must be included explicitly. No implementation may silently equate ARX velocity with the derivative of its position output.

Any RAPT transfer must specify observed/action features, normalization, objective, recurrent initialization, calibration formula and exact author revision. Released scoring/gates and manuscript formulas are separate documented variants. No LLM diagnosis, paid service or physical controller action is required for the detection comparison.
