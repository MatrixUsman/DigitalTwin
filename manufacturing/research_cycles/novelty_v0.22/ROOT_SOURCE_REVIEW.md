## Closest newly identified mathematical comparator

**Ghanipoor, Murguia, Ferrari Trecate and van de Wouw, arXiv:2504.19688v1, 28 April 2025.** [Version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19688), [full author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.19688v1). Read §§II–III, Theorem 1, Remark 1 and conclusion. The sensor-fault filter bank uses robust recurrent equilibrium networks and synthetic fault signals generated from healthy data. Eq.6 bounds output differences from above in truncated l2 norm; the authors distinguish a necessary sensitivity condition from sufficient insensitivity to other faults. Their conclusion leaves sufficient sensitivity and actuator/process extensions for future work. These are version-specific observations, not a defect claim.

**Published counterpart:** *Provably robust data-driven sensor fault diagnosis using incrementally stable recurrent equilibrium networks*, Automatica 191, 113096, September 2026, DOI [10.1016/j.automatica.2026.113096](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.automatica.2026.113096). [Publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0005109826002803) was search-readable but direct open returned 403; [EPFL record](https://graphsearch.epfl.ch/en/publication/64388483-db0e-4b11-ae49-ebd0ba9075b2) supports publication identity. Final equation-level correspondence remains unresolved. An attempted arXiv v2 returned a cache miss; the actual version history lists only v1. Do not project the v1 future-work statement onto the final article. `FINAL_VERSION_CHECK.md` records the separate follow-up.
