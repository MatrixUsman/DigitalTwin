## Executable comparison set

The **recorded-data track** retains the three saved independent Edge-architecture seeds, mean, persistence and AR, and all four v0.4 score variants. No rerun or exposure is needed for this milestone. The **physical simulation track** uses freshly fitted normal-only AR/ARX, persistence, corrected observer error, its rolling45/CUSUM controls, direct command-reference error and fused error, with ordinary hysteresis ablations. It makes no paper-baseline score claim.

Every context-aware method gets the same approved command and validity flag. Every method gets the same noisy joint-state observations and scoring support. Labels and true plant parameters are evaluator-only. This lets us attribute benefits to protected context, prediction or alarm persistence instead of giving the candidate extra information invisibly. No fixed number of papers justifies combining incompatible tasks into a ranking.

**Remaining paper-comparison work:** the original contribution paper still needs a mechanism-specific comparison once a non-equivalent contribution is specified. Edge-family reproduction is partial; CASPER rights/lineage, ModernTCN transfer and close recovery-paper full text remain separate work. Current decisions are explicit rather than pending placeholders.
