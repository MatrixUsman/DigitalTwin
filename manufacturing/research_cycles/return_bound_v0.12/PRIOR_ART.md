Searches: exact Kaisar title with “recovery”; exact Howard title; exact RAPIO title with “recovery”. Publisher/author records were preferred; secondary summaries were not treated as method evidence. Failed retrieval is an access limitation, not evidence that a paper or mechanism does not exist. Search/metadata time is not a publication date.

The implemented rule combines a Gaussian linear-combination variance, triangle inequality, bounded temporal variation and a union bound over fixed windows/channels/time. These are standard tools. It also reduces to the previously specified iid control when rho=0 and K=1. We admit it as a **comparison control**, not our proposed original method. The multiwindow envelope can improve feasibility compared with a single conservative window without making the underlying mathematics novel.

A future contribution would need a specific, independently justified way to obtain useful command-dependent bias/noise/drift information from permitted commissioning observations, and a clear distinction from interval observers and existing recovery gating. Reference bias and physical deviation are not separately identifiable from one residual without additional assumptions. This study cannot establish those assumptions for CASPER or for a robot. The missing Kaisar full method remains a barrier to an exact novelty comparison, but does not prevent implementing a standard control honestly.
