## Diagnosis and hypothesis

The immutable v0.5 experiment is exposed development evidence. `DIAGNOSIS.json` records 565 benign alarms for its fused dwell rule, with 512 within one second of a discontinuity in the approved command. On authorized-offset episodes the deterministic mismatch energy exceeds sensor-noise energy. This association motivates, but does not prove, a transient-mismatch explanation.

Hypothesis: command-driven uncertainty allowances can reduce benign transient entries, while a separate, fixed physical-return requirement can prevent an expanded allowance from erasing an existing alarm. Compare a sampled model envelope with a nominal model and a command-mode adaptive threshold, then isolate recovery logic. No model learns from evaluation measurements. Measurements correct only the following observer's state.

It can contain impossible cross-channel combinations, fail between sampled models, and cannot enclose unbounded Gaussian noise with certainty.

Candidate: enter when E>h; retain until ten consecutive valid samples satisfy |y_j-r_nom,j|+3 sigma_j <= 0.1 training_std_j for every channel, with no new entry during that dwell. The nominal reference is the bank's middle member. This noise margin is a design convention, not a sequential probability guarantee or safety certificate.
