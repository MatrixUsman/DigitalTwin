# Close-prior-art check — 17 October 2026

This is a bounded mechanism review using primary sources, not a systematic review or novelty certificate. Search terms included interval observer adaptive threshold model uncertainty, mode-dependent fault detection, and recovery/clearance CUSUM. Recent learning papers remain separately admitted in earlier comparator registers; these theoretical controls are independently implemented and are not faithful reproductions of those papers.

The v0.5 prior-art register also retains Kaisar's 2026 DT/CUSUM recovery lead (full manuscript unresolved) and classical CUSUM/hysteresis. The present candidate separates an entry envelope from a fixed nominal-return criterion. This is algebraically different from v0.5 fused dwell hysteresis, but that fact establishes neither originality nor superiority. The admissible claim after a successful experiment is a measured trade-off for this specified virtual plant family. A publishable original contribution still needs a sharper distinction from interval-observer/recovery work and broader independent evidence.
