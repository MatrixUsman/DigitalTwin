## What this experiment can establish

A finite, recorded search can show whether reference error is reducible with ordinary linear models and whether a fixed clearance inequality is empirically reachable on new normal commands. It cannot establish an algorithmic difference from these methods. The fitted references must be available equally to ordinary alarm controls in any later experiment. Increasing ARX order, tuning a ridge penalty, using FIR, or adding a normal-only admission check does not by itself satisfy the user's original-method contribution objective.

A future proposed mechanism must specify information available at the decision time, how it distinguishes reference inadequacy from a persistent command-corruption effect, a concrete difference from closest recovery/interval methods, and a fair same-reference ablation. That mechanism remains unspecified; this study supplies or rejects its reference-model foundation. Full paper-level comparator reproduction and factual author approval remain separate unresolved tasks. No author communications or submission are authorized by this source check.
