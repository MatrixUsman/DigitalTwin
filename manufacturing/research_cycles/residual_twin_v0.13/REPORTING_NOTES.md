# Reporting clarifications after the pilot

These notes clarify wording without editing the pre-run freeze, code or results.

1. The frozen plan calls the four diagnostic starts “forced-latch probes.” The implementation tests ten consecutive return-evidence points within 250 observations; it does **not** include entry-threshold interruption inside that probe. Report `probe_success` as **return-evidence availability**, not as actual alarm clearance or post-attack recovery. The separately reported `alarm_points` does use the complete entry-priority alarm machine. No primary reference metric is affected.
2. The integrated normal observation interface requires complete command context and rejects gaps. The isolated gate has tested unknown-context behavior, but this does not constitute an end-to-end missing-context deployment test.
3. The Classens et al. row in frozen PRIOR_ART.md is too broad. The [institutional final published manuscript](https://pure.tudelft.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/244779014/1-s2.0-S0947358025000676-main.pdf) was accessed on 17 October 2026. Its exact title is *Recursive identification of structured systems: An instrumental-variable approach applied to mechanical systems*. It develops online estimation with an additive continuous-time model and a recursive refined instrumental-variable/block-coordinate procedure, including a flexible-beam example. It is relevant to structured identification and interpretable monitoring; it is not evidence that this paper already proposes our alarm-recovery rule. Accepted 21 April 2025; online 13 May 2025; DOI 10.1016/j.ejcon.2025.101238. No numerical ranking against it is asserted.
4. All 81 episodes are synthetic parameterizations of the existing simulator. Three noise seeds on the same task/plant are paired computational variations, not independent robots. No new public recording, attack validation, publication novelty or scientific significance test was produced.
