# Prior-art screen for the manufacturing question

Date: **16 October 2026**. Scope: a bounded primary-source check to inform the choice of a feasible question and threat model for **original CASPER v3**. This is not an exhaustive novelty search, a completed systematic review, or baseline admission. Applied the installed academic-research-suite's source-verification guidance; retained the workspace's engineering evidence rules. No model, source script, dataset acquisition, author contact or external service was executed. No human-reading attestation is implied.

## Decision-relevant finding

**External IMU forecasting, periodic alignment, task segmentation, context-sensitive thresholds and twin residuals already have close precedents.** A feasible research question must identify a specific failure mechanism and test it against those precedents. Renaming a predictor a twin, adding phase estimates, or reporting a higher headline F1 would not establish a contribution.

One candidate for the parent question/threat-model decision is whether an observer that repeatedly aligns itself to observed motion can accommodate a manipulated speed so well that its residual stops exposing the deviation. A prospective comparison could test **motion mismatch alone versus motion mismatch plus abnormal phase progression/dwell evidence**, with equal observations and calibration. This is an inference and proposed experiment, **not an observed weakness in the cited methods or an established literature gap**. Original CASPER provides speed interventions relevant to that narrow question; it does not establish independently labelled authorized changeovers, faults versus attacks, or a complete adversarial campaign.

**Scope decision received during this screen:** the parent is selecting a restricted command/application-speed threat and a right-arm **joint-position/joint-velocity plus native timestamp-difference** route, with monitoring telemetry/time assumed intact. The external-IMU path remains a separate reproduction track. Accordingly, this note retains IMU literature as overlap/context evidence; it does not require using IMU as the chosen twin's sole observation. The trusted-monitoring assumption must be explicit: the selected route does not address a controller compromise that can forge the same telemetry the detector receives.

## Boundaries to carry into the question

1. **Use the observed motion signal as the monitored object.** The selected controller route can model measured joint coordinates under its intact-telemetry assumption. It does not independently authenticate those coordinates, prove malicious intent, or validate controller-to-IMU synchronization. The separate external-IMU route cannot claim reconstructed joint angles without additional identification/validation evidence.
2. **Do not allow alignment to erase the quantity being tested.** If speed deviation is the target, retaining only an amplitude residual after arbitrary retiming may remove relevant evidence. Test this possibility, rather than assuming it occurs. A progression component must be causal and scored before current-observation state assimilation.
3. **A state observer must do identifiable work.** Specify retained joint-state/phase estimates, predictions, observation updates and uncertainty. The chosen controller route has supported boot-relative time, but not a verified shared wall clock; phase labels still are inferred rather than provider ground truth. An IMU-only alternative would instead operate in sample order without precise elapsed-time claims. Twin terminology requires a defensible model/coupling definition and validation; measured coordinates or a dashboard alone do not establish it.
4. **Keep parameter learning fixed initially.** A narrow state-estimation comparison is feasible without importing the water project's contaminated-adaptation question. Any later online parameter update is a distinct intervention requiring its own protocol and overlap search.
5. **Use the same normal-only information.** Compare a causal template/progression control, a matched predictor and the proposed observer under frozen preprocessing/normal-only calibration. Porting the CASPER predictor from IMU to controller features is a new evaluation, not a faithful reproduction of CASPER's published experiment. Imported methods require an explicit implementation and common-protocol label. Selection must never optimize against the eight held-out deviation episodes.
6. **Treat labels and generalization as limitations.** Controller flags are scheduled intervention records rather than verified physical onset; IMU labels are a separate retrospective layer. There are no verified independent task/run IDs. Test outcomes can support a bounded single-recording result, not all-manufacturing robustness, malicious intent, physical safety or a leading-journal acceptance claim. Boundary sensitivity must be preregistered, not selected for attractive scores.

The open research question is whether the proposed state/progression mechanism yields a useful, explainable trade-off after fair controls. This screen does **not** show that it will, or that no existing method does the same thing. Before a novelty statement, search the exact frozen estimator/update/score mechanism and acquire the closest relevant manuscripts/code where lawful.
