## Decision

The generic constrained-observer/quarantine/recovery combination in [the design](../CONTRIBUTION_DESIGN_V0.2.md) is **not admitted as a novel contribution**. It is a candidate to compare against existing methods. A specific, demonstrably distinct recovery condition might remain worth studying, but the current search does not establish one. Do not advertise a new algorithm, theorem, superiority or final title from these notes.

The next task must compare full equations, assumptions, reset/recovery criteria and code where available, while establishing a method-level limitation of a corrected CASPER-family comparator. Different application sectors alone do not establish novelty. The recent-paper window applies to our main comparison roster, not to older prior art that can invalidate a novelty claim.
