# Replacement-comparator screen and transfer recommendation

Audit date: **17 October 2026, Asia/Karachi**. Project: manufacturing; owner Haiqa Mushtaq. This is a bounded primary-source and static-code study using the installed academic-research-suite. It is not a systematic review, a human-reading attestation, an executed model, or evidence that the search found every eligible article. No raw CASPER observations, checkpoints, training notebooks or attack scripts were executed.

## Decision

**This search did not establish three recent published methods evaluated on our exact original-CASPER v3 right-arm, normal-only task.** Preserve that unresolved result. Do not turn related datasets, different sensors or methods that could be transferred into a claim that three papers already share our experiment.

For a defensible comparison, retain the ordinary velocity, periodic-template and causal-predictor controls, and recommend **ModernTCN (ICLR 2024) as one explicitly transferred, modified author-code detector**. Its manuscript, implementation and MIT license are available. Its published experiment does not use CASPER, and its unmodified evaluation violates our protocol. This recommendation is a documented extension of the candidate search to a general time-series method; it does not silently satisfy the existing published-same-dataset criterion. Accepting the transfer track must be recorded in the project's comparator decision. The adapter, environment and tests still need implementation admission before training.

The proposed matched synchronization variants remain necessary: the ordinary controls and an imported neural detector do not isolate the effect of preserving nominal progression. Conversely, the matched variants alone cannot establish an advantage over competent ordinary detection.

**Proposed transfer specification, not an executed configuration:** use the same twelve q/qdot channels permitted to other main methods, a train-fitted external scaler, and trailing windows of 100 observations within valid segments. At time t calculate the mean squared difference between the standardized current observation and its reconstructed endpoint; do not use future observations. Keep the common 1,528-observation warmup and complete eligible support even though the model needs shorter history. Relative time may enforce gaps and alignment but is not an input to this initial neural variant. Evaluation-mode batch normalization, frozen weights and fixed external scaling are required during replay; per-window RevIN uses only the available trailing context and must not accumulate test-wide statistics.

The training objective may reconstruct all positions of a **training-only** window; that does not expose final observations. Checkpoint selection may use the designated normal development loss, after a finite architecture/epoch/seed policy is specified. The published code's checkpoint selection and threshold path must not be called. All final stochastic seeds must follow the project policy rather than its default 2021 seed. Changes to endpoint alignment, dataset, features, split, calibration, seeds and evaluation belong in the deviation ledger. The resulting method is a **modified author-code comparator on CASPER**, never a faithful reproduction of the paper's reported score.

Required implementation gates: a label-free model interface; causal endpoint/alignment tests; future-perturbation invariance; gap/split isolation; train-only scaler fitting; calibration/final access rejection during training; no runtime update of fitted parameters; complete finite eligible scores; pinned dependency validation and bounded resource pilot. The repository's unpinned `requirements.txt` and README Python 3.7 suggestion do not establish compatibility with our current Python 3.15 environment. A separate project-local dependency environment must be proposed and validated before use. No installation was performed in this study.

**Integration review clarification:** this proposed endpoint score conditions on the current sample and is a reconstruction, not a forecast formed only from past state. The frozen evaluation protocol's past-state prediction sentence needs explicit interface reconciliation before execution admission. No protocol was changed in this audit. See the [central admission gate](../COMPARATOR_ADMISSION.md); a no-future-input test alone cannot establish past-only forecasting.

## Edge-2025 reserve: what was resolved

The complete author PDF has an unreplaced LaTeX template running header; the IEEE DOI and institutional publication record establish its actual identity. Do not derive its date from the header. The manuscript excludes anomaly recordings from regression training, but describes threshold grid search and evaluates several aggregation-window sizes (pp. 5–6 and 10–11). This makes it a useful architectural source, not an unchanged clean comparator.

At the pinned MIT successor repository, [`notebooks/main.ipynb`](https://github.com/hkayann/Real-time-Anomaly-Detection-in-Industrial-Robotic-Arms-via-TinyML/blob/41b907749b7e5882319690a627a251e93fa99982/notebooks/main.ipynb), zero-based source-cell indices:

- **3:** original-data nominal 60/20/20 split and training-only StandardScaler.
- **6, 12, 22–27:** original `nicla.csv`/`right_arm.csv` loading and retrospective IMU annotation. These are not successor-release identity proof.
- **235:** successor nominal 60/20/20 slicing and training-only StandardScaler.
- **247/249, 262/264/266/268, 283/285:** 1,000 candidate thresholds across combined normal/anomalous scores, retaining the best F1 using labels; several rolling-window sizes are compared.

None of those cells was executed. The mixed notebook and public license resolve availability and permission for that pinned successor code, **not** the original repository's license or exact correspondence to each published run. Porting its predictor would still need a named model block, complete preprocessing/score alignment, new fixed normal-only calibration and a disclosed sensor/dataset change.
