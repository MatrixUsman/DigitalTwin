## Finding that governs the bounded investigation

Command-conditioned thresholds, learned nominal-error intervals, set-membership decisions, observer-consistency recovery, hysteresis and dwell already exist. A frozen command-conditioned envelope is a bounded hypothesis for transient/mismatch effects, but naming or combining those ingredients does not establish an original detector. The useful prospective question is whether a precisely specified inference rule reduces benign alarm burden while preserving evidence of unresolved physical deviation, and whether that advantage survives equally informed ordinary controls. The matched replay subsequently identifies noise-triggered entry and dwell amplification as the dominant present limitation; the candidate must face simple controls for that explanation.

There is no basis here for claiming superiority over Kaisar, CASPER, FAIL-Detect or a general state of the art. Their tasks and information differ. Any proposed numerical adaptation must be labelled as our implementation under a modified protocol.

## One bounded candidate worth investigating

**Diagnosis update before candidate development:** the saved [v0.18 replay summary](../../research_cycles/review_extension_v0.18/runs/replay_002/summary.json) records 1,198 versus 1,195 benign alarm points for base/RBF no-dwell, both 108 fresh/overlap detections and zero outside-tolerance inactive points. Aggregating [its diagnostic records](../../research_cycles/review_extension_v0.18/runs/replay_002/diagnostics.json) gives 853 base instantaneous noisy exceedances versus 150 noiseless-reference exceedances, and 5,958 base held points despite available clearance evidence. The [verification receipt](../../research_cycles/review_extension_v0.18/VERIFICATION.json) passes. These are exposed exploratory diagnostic results, not a new original-contribution result. They weaken the earlier presumption that reference error is the dominant failure: noise entry followed by persistence amplification is the stronger explanation. Legitimate-offset boundary effects remain a secondary condition to examine. A contextual envelope must beat a simple declared noise/persistence control to earn an added-component argument.

**Candidate hypothesis: a frozen command-conditioned nominal-error envelope, with separate evidence for deviation and return.** Retain the protected command-driven reference. Fit any center and width using clean commissioning data only; calibrate on a disjoint normal set. Conditioning variables may include authorized-command increments and their causal history, but never measured residual magnitude at inference, attack labels, future commands, evaluator state, or a changing “normal” model learned during attack. The exact feature basis and number of configurations must be fixed by the execution protocol.

The following is our derivation of the desired semantics, not an original theorem. Let the normalized observed reference residual be

\[
e_{t,j}=d_{t,j}+b_j(c_t)+\eta_{t,j},\qquad
z_{t,j}=e_{t,j}-\hat b_j(c_t),
\]

where \(d\) is physical deviation from the uncorrupted counterpart, \(c_t\) is permitted command context, and the combined nominal-reference/measurement error is assumed bounded by \(a_j(c_t)\) after centering. **Only the evaluator knows \(d\).** If that assumption holds, interval subtraction gives

\[
L_t=\max_j (|z_{t,j}|-a_j(c_t))_+,
\qquad U_t=\max_j (|z_{t,j}|+a_j(c_t)),
\qquad L_t\leq\|d_t\|_\infty\leq U_t.
\]

For a declared physical tolerance \(\varepsilon\), \(L_t>\varepsilon\) supports “outside,” \(U_t\leq\varepsilon\) supports “inside,” and the remainder is unresolved. A state machine can retain an already active alarm while unresolved and clear only on the declared inside evidence. Unknown is a reported outcome and cost, not a successful recovery classification. The same inference support, censoring and calibration population must apply to controls.

These triangle-inequality/set-inversion operations are standard. A normal quantile envelope does **not** establish the bound under attack, plant shift or unseen command context. Pointwise empirical coverage is neither a simultaneous guarantee nor proof of physical safety. If averages replace instantaneous residuals, the interval bounds an averaged deviation; it cannot justify instantaneous physical return without an explicit variation bound. Sign cancellation and complete post-initialization window readiness must be tested. Out-of-support command context must abstain or use a declared conservative fallback, never silently extrapolate a “safe” certificate.

The candidate is attractive only if the dominant observed failure comes from predictable command-dependent reference error. If the matched control diagnoses dwell as the dominant cause, an envelope may add unnecessary complexity. A causal FIR correction driven by command history is a reasonable *ordinary predictor control* if needed, not a second novelty claim or license for an open-ended architecture search.

### Minimum comparisons and falsification

1. Matched base and RBF with and without dwell, preserving the original audit.
2. Constant nominal-error envelope with the same two-sided rule, to isolate conditioning.
3. The same contextual residual/envelope with ordinary threshold hysteresis, to isolate the recovery semantics from better calibration.
4. A contextual entry rule with the pre-existing return rule, if necessary to locate the source of any gain.
5. A simple fixed physical-tolerance point rule and a predeclared finite-support entry rule (for example three of five past exceedances), to test whether ordinary noise rejection explains the gain. They receive the same observations and have their delay/brief-event costs reported.

Report occupancy, starts/episodes, detection/misses/delay, outside-tolerance inactive time, return/clearance timing, unknown fraction/duration, envelope coverage and runtime. An apparent gain that merely moves false alarms into unreported unknown intervals is a failure. Calibration may determine the declared normal operating point, not select models using final attacks. Whole-episode or condition blocks—not rows—are the evidence units for coverage summaries and paired uncertainty.

Before fresh confirmation, freeze the finite candidate set and chosen artifacts, concrete normal-calibration support, new task/plant condition families, primary benefit, maximum acceptable costs and a stop rule. A successful experiment could support a narrowly stated useful mechanism in this simulated monitoring setting. It would not by itself resolve originality, full-paper comparator reproduction, real-robot generalization, or publication suitability.
