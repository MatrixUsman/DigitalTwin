Corrected VOR V1.1 dated 5 February 2025. Original source revision `bff03c91d155bf3134f6d801d3a4f4af7ea78adb`; [previous full audit](literature/CASPER_ADMISSION_2026-10-17.md). Normal-only prediction on external IMU, not our earlier controller-input observer. Its checkpoint/run lineage and original code reuse terms remain unresolved. Original source is inspected, not copied into the new harness.

**Closest successor (Edge-2025):** same authors, *Real-Time Anomaly Detection for Industrial Robotic Arms Using Edge Computing*, IEEE Internet of Things Journal 12(15), 29696–29712, DOI [10.1109/JIOT.2025.3569780](https://doi.org/10.1109/JIOT.2025.3569780), 1 August 2025 issue. The author manuscript describes an additional three-task collection, compact predictors, aggregation-window trade-offs and edge deployment. See [author manuscript](https://iotgarage.net/publications/pdfs/Kayan2025a.pdf). First-online day was not independently resolved in this cycle; the PDF's template header is not a publication date.

The [successor repository](https://github.com/hkayann/Real-time-Anomaly-Detection-in-Industrial-Robotic-Arms-via-TinyML/tree/41b907749b7e5882319690a627a251e93fa99982) has an inspected MIT grant. Revision `41b907749b7e5882319690a627a251e93fa99982` is pinned, not asserted to be the latest head today. Its notebook contains several original/successor and model-size branches. Public models are 8,772-byte CNN and 9,608-byte LSTM artifacts. Exact links between notebook execution, scaler, model file and each published table remain unresolved. The collected requirements omit TensorFlow; the new Python 3.12/TensorFlow 2.16.2 environment is ours, not a reconstruction of the authors' historical environment.

The analytical check for M1 is elementary, not a new theorem. For independent zero-mean residual noise with standard deviation σ and an additive residual shift A lasting L samples, a trailing sum of width w has peak mean displacement `A min(L,w)` and noise standard deviation `σ sqrt(w)`. Thus a long window can dilute a short shift relative to its noise. Real residual dependence, nonlinear scores and threshold calibration require empirical assessment; this calculation supplies neither CASPER F1 nor a new detector.

## What was actually reproduced

- **13 initial + 5 additional synthetic tests**, now 18 passing checks, validate source diagnostics, future-target alignment, strict shapes/nonfinite handling, train-only scaling and separate window support. These are software checks.
- **Released-model execution:** both published TFLite files loaded and produced finite outputs. This is model-artifact compatibility, not the authors' full experiment or edge timing.
- **Normal-only pilot:** an independently coded 45-step compact CNN with the published architectural family fitted 8,192 targets and scored 2,048 separate development targets. One epoch, seed 17, batch 256. It is a subset feasibility implementation with explicit deviations, not a faithful reproduction. The [cycle result report](../research_cycles/contribution_v0.2/RESULTS.md) records actual results/resources/failures.

No attack F1, threshold, classifier ranking or complete paper-score reproduction was produced. The paper's main numerical claims remain neither reproduced nor disproved by these diagnostics.

## Decision

Use the corrected compact CNN as a feasible first **modified/reimplemented** forecasting comparator. Keep the released recurrent artifact as a state-policy diagnostic until its scaler, training branch and inference semantics are resolved. Retain simple persistence/AR, fixed/phase-aligned nominal references and ordinary multiscale/CUSUM controls. CASPER's full external-IMU reproduction remains conditional; TWINE/CatBoost do not become normal-only comparators merely because their collection names overlap.

The next candidate mechanism and prospective comparison are specified in [CONTRIBUTION_DESIGN_V0.2.md](CONTRIBUTION_DESIGN_V0.2.md). It is a hypothesis, not an admitted novel contribution. The lack of demonstrated method-level benefit and complete close-prior-art coverage prevents writing an original-contribution results section now.
