# Evidence needed for stronger manufacturing claims

28 October 2026. Planning/explanation only; no new experiment or successful contribution. User asked why consistent superiority and real-robot attack-detection accuracy cannot yet be claimed, and what would be required.

## Current barriers

The original ARX–RBF candidate gives only 0.26 percentage-point F1 gain on the original synthetic audit and a lower F1 on the later campaign. The contextual candidate reduces false alarms but fails delay/support constraints. The latest constrained correction fails normal-only advancement and is beaten by a clipped FIR on the recorded error/radius pair. These are actual scientific failures, not just missing presentation. The nominal real IMU study measures development prediction error; it cannot supply attack precision/recall. Training seeds and simulator parameterizations do not create independent robot acquisitions. Historical evaluated recordings and synthetic campaigns cannot become fresh confirmation for redesign informed by their outcomes.

## Proposed bounded next milestone

Audit real-recording compatibility without fitting models or opening a reserved test: exact release, authorized reuse, attack annotations and time alignment, independent recording/episode identifiers, eligible observation channels and access to genuine protected approved commands. Existing CASPER release/clock/annotation uncertainty must be resolved or explicitly delimit the claim. The current command-driven ARX–RBF mechanism cannot be transferred by treating attacked controller commands as trusted intentions. If required inputs are absent, choose a justified observation-only research question or another documented acquisition; never invent the missing command stream.

CASPER corrected author manuscript describes real robot velocity interventions and external IMU monitoring: https://iotgarage.net/publications/pdfs/Kayan2024a.pdf (opened 28 October 2026). Candidate source, not confirmation that our received release has aligned labels/eligible protected commands. Official voraus-AD repository and schema: https://github.com/vorausrobotik/voraus-ad-dataset/blob/main/README.md and https://github.com/vorausrobotik/voraus-ad-dataset/blob/main/voraus_ad.py (checked 28 October 2026). Its fault/anomaly categories do not by themselves establish cyberattack ground truth; MVT-Flow is an offline candidate, not automatically a causal online comparator.

## Evidence sequence

1. Produce an input/label/recording manifest and a claim-to-data eligibility decision. Distinguish offline recorded-robot detection, physical recovery monitoring, and live deployment.
2. Freeze recording/episode-level training, development and final evaluation roles before windows. Reserve genuinely unexposed evidence where possible. An already exposed record remains exploratory; if no suitable untouched acquisition exists, broad independent validation remains unavailable.
3. Establish strong classical and compatible recent published controls under the same inputs, labels and evaluation population. Disclose reproduction versus reimplementation, supervised versus normal-only access, online versus whole-sequence access, tuning budget and stopping. Never select thresholds using final attack labels.
4. Demonstrate a specific baseline limitation on development material; propose and assess a mechanism against close prior art and strongest simple controls. Do not presume additional neural complexity or training will solve the observed failure. Freeze ablations and useful effect/delay/false-alarm criteria before final outcomes.
5. Evaluate frozen candidates once on reserved data. Report per-event detection, precision/recall/F1, false alarms per normal operating hour, detection delay and misses. If recovery is claimed, require independent physical-return evidence and retained-alarm measurements. Quantify uncertainty at independent recording/episode level; very few events cannot support tight generalization claims. Report failures and all planned variants.

Real-recording evaluation can use an eligible public dataset without owning a robot; it supports claims limited to those recorded conditions. Live deployment needs a physical testbed, causal end-to-end timing and operational evaluation. Cross-robot claims require separate robots; multiple seeds do not substitute. No fixed accuracy target, publication outcome, elapsed-time estimate or funding requirement is established by this plan. Training/resource estimates follow a bounded compatibility pilot, not speculation.
