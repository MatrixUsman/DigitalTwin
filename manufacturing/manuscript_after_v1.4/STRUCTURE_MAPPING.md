## Limits on imitation

- Use the logical organization and level of explanation, not copied prose, figures, typography branding, authors, funding or performance claims.
- Keep our problem and information access: four-state command-aware simulation is not multilabel biomedical classification. Attention, label embeddings and transformers are not inserted merely to resemble the example.
- Keep negative and null findings visible. The proposed correction failed its declared target and the short window changed no decisions.
- Do not infer independent replication, statistical significance, physical safety, novelty or superiority from a more detailed layout.
- The example is not a protocol authority. For instance, its training count in prose (29,960, p. 5) differs from Table 1 (24,960, p. 6). Our data counts must follow our own saved records, not copied table patterns. This observation is not a complete audit of the example paper.

The research skill was used for bounded manuscript revision and evidence traceability; the PDF skill for layout, rendering and verification. No external writing/detector service or full automated multi-agent pipeline was used.
