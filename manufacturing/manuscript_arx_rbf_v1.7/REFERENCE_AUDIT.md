## Claim limits

- The primary reference-correction and attack/recovery improvement criteria failed. No reference is used to disguise that result as a novel validated detector.
- CASPER/Edge-style results concern inspected independent implementations and normal prediction. They do not establish published-system superiority.
- No recent forecasting paper is presented as an executed baseline merely to enlarge the reference list.
- voraus-AD labels and execution counts are locally verified metadata; FactoryNet totals remain attributed author reports. Neither establishes cyberattack intent or physical-return annotations.
- “Independent data” means an acquisition distinct from CASPER, not independent rows or episodes from the same robot. Imported copies are not counted twice.
- The main text makes no claim that the literature search is exhaustive or that missing access proves an absent prior method.
