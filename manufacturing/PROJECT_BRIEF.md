# Manufacturing research project

Last updated: 17 October 2026. Project owner: **Haiqa Mushtaq**. Ownership is an organizational assignment, not a decision about academic authorship.

This is an independent academic research project on cybersecurity of manufacturing cyber–physical systems. It has progressed through dataset/source audits, bounded software validation and full-training normal-only selection of three ordinary controls; a bounded empirical motion-observer validation is now complete, while physical-twin claims and the final comparison remain unresolved. The original CASPER archive and five members have been acquired with integrity checks and hashes; a corrected CASPER manuscript, pinned code/documents and explicitly author-reported tables/figures are stored locally. A [study guide](docs/DATA_AND_BASELINE_STUDY.md) connects full controller-schema validation, annotation lineage and paper-to-code findings. No paper baseline has been reproduced and no attack-detection performance exists; normal-development selection losses and technical resource measurements are recorded.

## Provisional direction

Working title: **“Correction Evidence in Robotic Motion Monitoring: A CASPER Case Study.”** The [contribution/scope decision](docs/CONTRIBUTION_AND_EVIDENCE_SCOPE.md) narrows the planned contribution to a controlled single-acquisition score comparison. Phase tracking, correction surprise and tracking/detection trade-offs are established prior art. The earlier observer/digital-twin titles remain historical motivation; the [normal-only validation](docs/OBSERVER_VALIDATION_REPORT.md) supports an empirical observer, not a validated physical twin or security benefit.

Current operational question: **Does adding measurement-correction discrepancy improve the same empirical motion observer's detection of recorded right-arm speed changes compared with residual-only, conventional surprise/energy scores and simple controls under the same normal-only calibration?** R already contains nominal progression. This scope refinement leaves the frozen threat, inputs, annotations, splits and evaluation rules unchanged; detection benefit remains unmeasured. The [core roster](docs/COMPARISON_ROSTER_DECISION.md) has eleven variants: seven implemented and four required C/K/L/Rv extensions still at design stage.

The [question/threat-model decision](docs/RESEARCH_QUESTION_AND_THREAT_MODEL.md) selects a **normal-only right-arm controller-telemetry study** using joint positions, joint velocities and relative elapsed time. The restricted adversary changes motion commands; telemetry/time delivered to an assumed protected monitor remain truthful. No authentication or resistance to fully compromised controller firmware is established by the dataset. Model parameters/reference/calibration stay fixed during replay; the subject is state synchronization, distinct from water's provisional parameter-adaptation question.

The earlier question about attacks versus authorized changeovers and faults is deferred because the original collection lacks the required independent ground truth. The [current comparator decision](docs/COMPARATOR_ADMISSION.md) keeps CASPER's external-IMU reproduction separate and held. Right-arm simple velocity/template/AR controls are now fitted and selected; a **CASPER-inspired independent CNN** is a design candidate, not a faithful reproduction or permission to copy unlicensed source. TWINE and CatBoost are background only for Group D; their supervised side track is not admitted. ModernTCN 2024 is a conditional **modified author-code transfer candidate**, requiring a common-input causal adapter and validation. It is not a published CASPER baseline or execution-ready model. Novelty remains unverified; see the [dated overlap screen](docs/literature/RQ_PRIOR_ART_SCREEN_2026-10-16.md).

The distinction between an attack, a fault, and an authorized transition must come from documented ground truth. “Cyber-physical,” predictive maintenance, or generic ICS branding alone does not establish cybersecurity relevance or manufacturing data. A simulator, a detector dashboard, and a state-coupled digital twin are different artifacts and must be described accurately. One repetitive acquisition with eight scheduled intervals supports only a focused case study. The [independent-evidence plan](docs/INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE_PLAN.md) prioritizes a separate voraus-AD physical-fault/variable-execution audit and NIST benign-condition evidence; neither supplies cyber labels. No additional dataset is admitted. Broad cross-robot cybersecurity claims remain withheld.

## Scope and decision criteria

- Screen main comparative papers published from **16 October 2023 through 16 October 2026**, preferably 2024–2026. Record online, issue, and preprint dates separately; keep older background separate.
- Select manufacturing-specific data and comparable protocols. SWaT and WADI are not imposed on this project. Do not reuse water findings as manufacturing evidence.
- Require identifiable process semantics, attack labels or documented injection events, normal operation, and timing before claiming process-aware cyberattack detection can be evaluated.
- Establish what the proposed twin knows at each timestamp, how its state is synchronized, and what independent trusted information exists. A predictive model alone is not automatically a validated twin.
- Keep title, mechanism, comparison set, and contribution provisional until a faithful baseline and an explicit literature-gap audit support decisions.
